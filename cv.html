Profile

Design leader with 25 years in complex B2B and enterprise domains — telecom, broadcast, dealer management, industrial ERP. Currently design lead on a dealer management SaaS replacing three legacy platforms across 400+ dealerships, and building an AI-assisted design practice now used across a media group. I have deliberately stayed hands-on through three lead roles of increasing scope, building products and the organisations that make them — including a design function grown from zero and acquired for it, and an operating model that outlived my tenure. I own product definition alongside UX, and I stay in the detail, because in regulated, data-heavy domains the detail is the strategy.

Alma · Design Lead

Modernising a mature design organisation — a product operating model and a shift from handovers to overlapping co-working — while steering design on Alma's largest development undertaking to date.

Design lead on a dealer management SaaS replacing three legacy platforms serving 400+ Finnish dealerships. Own product definition and PRDs alongside UX/UI; line-manage one UX designer, who joined as a junior and has grown to intermediate through mentoring.
